# 📘 ExamPrep: An AI Skill for Exam-Ready Answers

Turn your college notes into answers a teacher would give full marks to.
Upload your notes, ask exam questions, and get complete, neatly structured
answers with every claim traced back to the exact page or slide.

## ✨ Features
- 📚 **Notes-first answers**: built from your own PDFs, slides and documents,
  with trusted web sources filling any gaps (and clearly flagged)
- 🎯 **Marks-aware length**: answers sized for 2, 5 and 10 mark questions
- 🧩 **Command-word aware**: define, explain, differentiate, derive and
  short-note questions each get the layout examiners expect
- 🧮 **Step-by-step numericals**: theory, formulas, and the *why* behind
  every step
- 🧠 **Scenario analysis**: justifies the chosen solution and explains why
  the alternatives were rejected
- 🖼️ **Real diagrams, no ASCII art**: points to the diagram in your notes
  or a reliable source
- 🔑 **Keyword checklist**: important technical terms listed at the end
  of every answer
- 📎 **Source citations**: e.g. `notes.pdf : pg 11-13`

## 🚀 Usage
1. Add `examprep.md` as a skill, or paste it at the start of a chat.
2. Upload your notes.
3. Ask your exam questions.
